# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community.

## Our Standards
Use inclusive language, respect different viewpoints, give constructive feedback, and act professionally. Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, and publishing private information without permission are unacceptable.

## Enforcement Responsibilities
Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards.

## Scope
This Code of Conduct applies to project spaces and situations where someone represents the project or its community.

## Enforcement
Unacceptable behavior may be reported to project maintainers. Maintainers may take appropriate corrective action.

## Enforcement Guidelines
Responses may include a warning, temporary restriction, or permanent removal from project participation, depending on the severity and context.
